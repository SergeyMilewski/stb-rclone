stb-rclone

stb-rclone is a personal, non-commercial tool. It uses rclone on the owner's own home device to access the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

  • The app only accesses the Google Drive of the person who signs in, and only on that person's own device.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's device.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
